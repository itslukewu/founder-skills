# Speaking Rubric — v0.1

Use this for personal **baseline and repeat comparisons**, not as a scientifically validated measure of speaking talent.

## Procedure
Record a 60–180 second talk without reading a script. The speaker and, optionally, a listener each score the five dimensions from **1 to 5**. Quote an observable passage or timestamp for each score. Re-score the next take using the same criteria.

| Dimension | 1 — Needs attention | 3 — Functional | 5 — Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Point clarity | No identifiable claim | Claim eventually identifiable | Claim explicit and memorable |
| Logical coherence | Reasons disconnected | Mostly relevant progression | Each reason/transition supports the point |
| Information selection | Major tangents/overload | Some unnecessary detail | Detail supports a single takeaway |
| Natural language | Mostly read or stilted | Understandable, uneven | Conversational, accessible phrasing |
| Delivery control | Frequent loss of thread | Some pauses or overruns | Recoverable pauses and effective pace |

Scores **2** and **4** fall between the descriptions. Don't treat a single numerical total as more informative than specific examples.

## Feedback rules
Distinguish an unclear argument from an unfamiliar audience; note nerves, recording conditions, and subject difficulty. Avoid scoring accent, personality, or speaking style as inherently deficient. Consent is required before sharing recordings or transcripts externally.
