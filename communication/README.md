# Communication

**From thoughts to clear, confident expression.**

Communication is **one Founder Skill**, not the scope or identity of the entire Founder Skills repository. This module focuses on independently initiating and organizing spoken explanations for cameras, meetings, and public speaking, particularly when answering a question is easier than starting a talk.

## Core loop

1. **Discover** a worthwhile topic from an observation, problem, decision, disagreement, or lesson.
2. **Extract** one defensible point (not just a broad subject).
3. **Structure** it using reasons, examples, and explicit logic.
4. **Speak** using keywords rather than a memorized script.
5. **Review** the recording and choose one improvement for the next attempt.

## Scope and future growth

The long-term conceptual learning areas are Foundations, Frameworks, Skills, Workflows, Practice, and Examples. Only populated paths are included in v0.1. Meeting and public-speaking workflows, additional frameworks, and verified examples may be added after observing real practice needs.

## Coaching principle

AI may ask diagnostic questions, propose alternate structure, and give evidence-based feedback. **It must not manufacture beliefs, personal experiences, performance claims, or details for the speaker.** Ask the speaker to choose the viewpoint and approve any substantive rewrite.
