# Camera Speaking Workflow — v0.1

## Goal
Deliver a clear **60–180 second unscripted-to-camera explanation** grounded in the speaker's own thinking. This workflow is for training independent speaking, not optimizing a promotional video.

## Inputs
Audience, approximate duration, one real observation or topic, shareability/privacy constraints, and the speaker's desired outcome.

## Workflow

### 1. Discover
Run [Topic Discovery](../03-skills/topic-discovery/SKILL.md). Choose one question worth answering. **Gate:** speaker chooses the topic.

### 2. Extract
Run [Point Extraction](../03-skills/point-extraction/SKILL.md). Convert the topic into a point and a likely listener takeaway. **Gate:** speaker confirms the claim reflects their view.

### 3. Structure
Use [PREP](../02-frameworks/prep.md) for a short point; allow a problem-first opening when needed. Prepare:
- One claim
- One to two supporting reasons
- One real example
- One implication

### 4. Prepare speaking cues
Write **three to five keywords**, not a word-for-word script. Set an approximate duration. Identify statements that need verification or qualification.

### 5. Record
Do one uninterrupted take if practical. Speak conversationally. If stuck, pause, summarize your last point, and return to the next cue rather than restarting automatically.

### 6. Review
Replay once and evaluate using the [Speaking Rubric](../05-practice/speaking-rubric.md). Note one concrete strength, one priority issue, and a measurable next attempt.

### 7. Repeat
Record a second take changing **only the priority issue**. Preserve authentic wording and compare both takes against the same rubric.

## Outputs
A topic, approved claim, cue card, recording/transcript (optional and private by default), scored self-review, and next drill.

## Guardrails
Do not fabricate experiences, evidence or metrics. Do not publish recordings without explicit approval. If using AI feedback, separate observable issues from subjective stylistic preferences. Do not let AI silently replace the speaker's conclusion.
